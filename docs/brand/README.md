# Shelf brand mark

- `shelf-logo-source.webp`: the logo as supplied by the user, on its cream background.
- `shelf-mark-512.png`: transparent master. The background was removed by an edge-connected fill, with the page stripes protecting the pages so they stay intact, and the edges anti-aliased.
- App files in `src/assets/brand/`:
  - `shelf-mark.png` (128px): the sidebar and top-bar mark, shown at 28px.
  - `favicon-48.png`: the browser-tab favicon.
  - `apple-touch-icon.png` (180px): the iOS home-screen icon, on the app's `#101413` surface.

All three app files use a dark-UI variant that lifts only the deepest cover greens (luminance below about 72), so the silhouette stays readable on the near-black chrome. Keep the mark at 20px or larger and give it clear space of at least a quarter of its width. Don't recolour it or place it on a busy image.
